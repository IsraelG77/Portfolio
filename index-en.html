02 · How I work

From the problem to a proposed change

Before choosing a tool, I examine what needs to be decided and which information can support that decision.

01

Understand what needs to change

Identify the decision, the people involved, and process constraints. Examine where delays, discrepancies, and unclear criteria occur.

02

Examine data and rules

Review sources, definitions, and assessment criteria. Seek measures and rules that can be explained, checked, and discussed with people who know the operation.

03

Propose and implement changes

The solution may be a revised workflow, a documented rule, an analysis, or an automation. Track results within the scope of each project.

03 · Projects

People problems and the solutions I built

Each project starts from an operational need. Demos use fictional data.

01

Internship selection — Next Gen

Assessing and tracking more than 17,500 applications stage by stage required consolidated information. I managed the selection process end to end and built an automated dataset that brings selection-stage information together, with stage-by-stage tracking and diversity and inclusion analysis.

Explore demo Fictional data
02

Performance calibration session planning

Organizing performance calibration sessions involves rules for workload, seniority, allocation, and business units. I developed a proof of concept, still in development, that applies these rules to estimate session distribution by region and period and flag potential planning bottlenecks.

Explore demo Fictional data
03

Internal mobility — timelines and bottlenecks

Tracking internal mobility across teams and regions required identifying the stages where delays occurred. I developed a dashboard that brings together more than 40 employee and vacancy variables to examine timelines, movements, and idle time across business units.

04

Recruitment campaigns — spend and conversion

Campaign prioritization needed to account for conversion to hire, not only application volume. I connected media spend, openings, regions, and hires to compare campaign outcomes and support budget allocation.

05

Hiring operations — capacity and forecasting

Prioritizing work and planning Talent Acquisition team capacity depended on a consistent view of the vacancy flow. I developed this monitoring, with consistent region and action definitions, processed-volume analysis, forecasts, and bottleneck identification.

06

Internship program — data and allocation

After approval, intern information was spread across separate datasets. I reconciled three data sources to check the approved population and track diversity, academic background, and allocation with consolidated information.
