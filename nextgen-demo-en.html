Next Gen: tracking 17,500+ applications stage by stage

My role in the real project: end-to-end management of the Next Gen internship selection process and development of the automated analysis workflow. The real program received over 17,500 applications; analysis time was reduced by 90%. This demo uses a separate set of 300 fictional applications to illustrate the interface. Assessment scores, demographic distributions, and outcomes are simulated and are not evidence about real candidates.
